v2.007: the Md debate, link check and weekly reporter

23 Oct 2026Addenda A41–A43Shadow mode: the Debate Signal is logged, not applied to Md

Md's last variable, the Debate Signal, now has its machinery: an evidence board and gate in code, news scouts, Debater A and B, the C&A decision models and a consensus rule in code. A reporter agent writes a weekly summary. A fix for the research run is included.
