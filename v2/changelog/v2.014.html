Data gaps and errors (Tsuchibot Main)

  • Could not be retrieved: failed requests by site, sources in error (debate evidence, marketing), pages whose layout changed.
  • Outdated: a market price older than 4 days, research older than 14 days, Google Trends data older than 35 days.
  • Cut short: time, iteration or site budgets reached, sites skipped after repeated failures, build phases not complete.
  • Severity and why: high = the valuation itself is missing or wrong; medium = computed but on less than it should; low = supporting data only; info = a permanent gap (nothing estimated in its place).

Checked

  • Live on a copy of the data: 136 marketing rows; every source worked except グランディハウス (503).
  • Order tables parsed for every year where the table exists; グランディハウス order counts are not kept because short counts run into their percentages in the filing text, but its amounts are exact.
  • Error report: 1 medium (5 of 37 universe members lack panel data), 3 low, 3 info on that copy.
  • Trends upload: classic and new export formats read; test mode and the mock dashboard: no errors.
