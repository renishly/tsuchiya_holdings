v2.006: M1 statistical comparables, panel backtest, research agent

23 Oct 2026Addenda A39–A40Adds reference rows; live weights change only after approval

The agent now builds a universe of listed Japanese housebuilders, fits two cross-sectional equations (M1) on about ten years of their filings, backtests every method against later returns and proposes weights. The proposal waits for the student's approval; until then M1 appears as reference rows and the combined value keeps equal weights.
