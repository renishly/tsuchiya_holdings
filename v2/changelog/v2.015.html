v2.015: price-store fix

30 Oct 2026Fix found by the new error report

The daily price store saved a mid-morning price as the day's close, and then mistook the next price move for a share split. Both are fixed, the bad rows were removed, and the error report now rates price problems correctly.

What happened

  • The store fetches prices once a day after the 15:30 close, or at any time when a company's history is missing. After the universe was widened, the 09:58 JST run on 30 Oct fetched the new companies' histories. Yahoo's bar for that morning was stored as the 30 Oct close for all 30 companies.
  • At 11:59 the morning price had moved by more than 1%. The store reads such a change as a share split, so it dropped the price history of 5 companies (タマホーム, アールプランナー, グッドコムアセット, ランディックス, 誠建設工業) for re-download.
  • The valuation on 30 Oct used Tsuchiya's 09:58 price (¥224) labelled as the close, and had no current price for タマホーム.

What changed

  • The store never saves a bar for today before 15:30 JST (nor any later date), so a morning price can no longer pass as a close or trigger a false split.
  • The 30 mid-morning rows were removed from raw/market/daily_prices.csv. The four Stock Price rows built from them are marked superseded, with a note, so the 29 Oct close (¥223) is used until the real 30 Oct close is stored. The 5 dropped histories are downloaded again at the next run.
  • Error report: irbank.net is a cross-check (low), not the source of the price. A missing or stale history for Tsuchiya or a peer in the price store is now reported (high for Tsuchiya or a missing history, medium for a stale peer).
