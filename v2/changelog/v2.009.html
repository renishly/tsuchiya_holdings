v2.009: a wider M1 universe

30 Oct 2026Addendum A45Takes effect at the next research run

M1 and the backtest compare Tsuchiya with more companies. The candidate list grows from 22 to 45 listed companies; the same residential-share screen decides which of them join, now with a fix for segments tagged without the usual name.

What to expect

  • Up to about 150 annual reports to find and download. The research run is resumable and may need two weekly runs to finish.
  • Companies listed only recently (e.g. ランディックス, 日本グランデ, スター・マイカ since 2019) have fewer years of history.
  • The universe becomes less purely "detached housebuilders". M1 still controls for profitability, growth and size, and the screen still excludes companies that are mainly non-residential.
