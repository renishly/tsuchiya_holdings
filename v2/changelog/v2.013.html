v2.013: your uploads become evidence

30 Oct 2026Addendum A49

Documents you upload to the Inbox of Tsuchibot Main or Tsuchibot C&A on the dashboard become citable evidence for the Md debate at the next run, and open the debate at once. This is the way to bring in a TDnet notice, which the agent may not fetch itself.

How to use it

  • Upload a PDF (with a text layer), an HTML page or a .txt/.md file to Main's or C&A's Inbox.
  • Optional note: the document's date and a ticker, e.g. 2026-10-29 1419 タマホーム 業績予想の修正. The rest of the note becomes the title. Without a date, the agent reads it from the first page, else uses the upload date.
  • At the next run, the activity log says "Inbox: stored <file> as evidence E-xxxxxxxx", the file leaves the Inbox, and the debate runs in that same run (trigger "student upload").
  • Other files (Word, Excel, scanned PDFs without text) stay in the Inbox and are reported once in the activity log.

What happens to an upload

  • Stored like a fetched source: the file, its text and its SHA-256 under raw/evidence/, a focused excerpt, and a check for instructions hidden in the text.
  • Shown on the C&A overview under "Your uploads" in "What the agent is watching", and in the debate record if used.
  • Kept on the evidence board for 120 days from the upload, whatever the document's own date.
