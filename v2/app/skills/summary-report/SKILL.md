1. Short paragraphs separated by blank lines, at most 500 words.
2. Cover: what changed; why the combined value moved; the Md debate (shadow or live) and what it decided; the
   research run and its approval state; what needs the student's attention.
3. Describe, do not decide. No trade language; no view on the share price; do not grade the debaters.
