---
name: valuation-combine
description: How the method prices are combined into one value with a range, and the weights used. Use to explain the
  Average Valuated Price, the weights or the range.
---
1. Read with `peek`: Average Valuated Price, Method weights, Valuation range low/high.
2. Weights: the student's approved backtest weights × a fallback factor (a method on a fallback basis counts half). Equal
   weights apply only when no approved weights exist. The Method weights row's formula says which rule was used and which
   parameter version.
3. Quote the methods used and their weights from the note.
