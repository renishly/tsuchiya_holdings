Methodology

Sources and precedence

有価証券報告書 XBRL > 有価証券報告書 PDF > 決算短信 > company website > third-party sites. Conflicts are recorded as validation notes, never resolved silently. Portals that forbid automated access (NIFTY不動産, 不動産ジャパン, ハトマークサイト, SUUMO, HOME'S, athome, kabutan, Yahoo!ファイナンス, minkabu) are never fetched; portal listings come only from the student's manual entries.

Validation checks

  • 総資産 = 負債 + 純資産; 売上高 − 売上原価 = 売上総利益; Σ segment external revenue = 売上高; cash roll-forward (期首 + 営業CF + 投資CF + 財務CF + 換算差額 = 期末).
  • Cross-document: the same fiscal year in consecutive 有報 must match unless a restatement/correction exists (both kept).
  • EPS plausibility (NI / average shares within 1%); unit/scale vs the 5-year summary (no ×1,000 or ×1,000,000 errors).
  • Cross-source: headline figures vs 決算短信; computed forward P/E vs irbank 予想PER within 10%.
  • Re-extraction: 10% of rows written each run are re-read from the cached file. Listings: duplicates flagged; every listing has a source URL.

Revenue-stream rules

取引態様 first: 売主 → 不動産事業: 売買 (住宅事業: 分譲 when the seller is 土屋ホーム); 専任/専属専任/一般媒介, 仲介, 代理 → 不動産事業: 仲介; rental with 貸主 → 賃貸事業; rental via 仲介 → 不動産事業: 仲介. No 取引態様 → Unmapped (never inferred). Homepage items are mapped by the page's business (展示場 → 住宅事業: 注文住宅; 分譲地 → 住宅事業: 分譲; ホームトピア → リフォーム事業; 土屋ホーム不動産 → 不動産事業: 仲介).

Legal penalty score (0–100)

(a) MLIT administrative dispositions against group entities, last 5 years: 50 × min(1, Σ severity / 4) with 免許/許可取消 = 4, 業務/営業停止 = 2, 指示 = 1, 行政指導/勧告 = 0.5. (b) News/court items naming a group entity in a brokerage legal context: 30 × min(1, n / 3). (c) Share of observed group brokerage sale listings under 専任/専属専任媒介: 10 × share. (d) Brokerage regulation in force, verified against primary sources: 10 × points / 10. Missing components are excluded and the available weight is stated.

This is an exposure measure, not a finding of wrongdoing.

Known caveats

  • List prices are not contract prices; a listing that disappears may have been withdrawn, not sold.
  • irbank prices are third-party daily closes; verify against JPX before relying on them.
  • Forward EBITDA assumes D&A equal to the latest actual year.
  • Segment definitions may change between years; check the segment note in each filing.
